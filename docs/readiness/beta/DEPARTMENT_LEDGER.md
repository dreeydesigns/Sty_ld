# STYLD department ledger

Branch `qoder/styld-beta-20261027`. One shared record for every department named in the
private-beta directive. Each row states what was found, what was done, and the evidence a
reader can check. Nothing here claims a journey is verified unless a named run proved it.

Status legend:
- FIXED: change is committed and covered by a named test or receipt.
- NOT A DEFECT: the report claim was wrong; the corrected reading is stated with evidence.
- MEASURED: the finding is quantified and recorded; a fix is proposed, not yet made.
- DEFERRED: intentionally out of beta scope under the scope-protection rules.
- BLOCKED: needs a founder action; independent work continued meanwhile.

  spinner, is the prerendered HTML. `/discover` and `/home` are the two feed surfaces; the
  pattern would repeat per client-rendered route, so the scope needs a founder decision rather
  than a drive-by refactor of 48 pages.

  so a byte proxy is no longer the missing piece. What is still missing is the provider side.

  account, a private CDN domain and signing key supplied, and `provider_restriction_verified`
  recorded per asset by whoever can prove it. Until then the route refuses, which is the
  truthful answer, not a working one. Existing assets remain public `image/upload` URLs.
