private-beta directive. Each row states what was found, what was done, and the evidence a
reader can check. Nothing here claims a journey is verified unless a named run proved it.

Status legend:
- FIXED: change is committed and covered by a named test or receipt.
- NOT A DEFECT: the report claim was wrong; the corrected reading is stated with evidence.
- MEASURED: the finding is quantified and recorded; a fix is proposed, not yet made.
- DEFERRED: intentionally out of beta scope under the scope-protection rules.
- BLOCKED: needs a founder action; independent work continued meanwhile.
