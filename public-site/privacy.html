What this site collects

Nothing automatically

This page sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, runs no script and loads no analytics or advertising code. There is no form here, so there is no database behind it.

One third-party request

The fonts come from Google Fonts. Loading them sends your IP address to Google, which is how a font server works. If that matters to you, the page still renders with your system fonts.

Email is your choice

The contact and demo links open your own email app with a message already written. Nothing is sent until you press send, and we only ever see what you choose to tell us in that message.

The beta is separate

The private beta application is not this site. It holds accounts, plans and images behind sign-in, and it is not reachable from any link on this page.
